Jamaica basketball returns to action in Managua from November 27 to 29, with three games in three days and two places in the next round up for grabs. The men's national team opens against Costa Rica before meeting Guyana and host Nicaragua.

## Jamaica's schedule

FIBA lists all three games at the Polideportivo Alexis Argüello. Managua is one hour behind Jamaica. All tip-offs below are in Jamaica time.

- **Friday, November 27 at 5:30 p.m.** — Jamaica vs. Costa Rica
- **Saturday, November 28 at 5:30 p.m.** — Guyana vs. Jamaica
- **Sunday, November 29 at 8 p.m.** — Nicaragua vs. Jamaica

## What Jamaica needs to advance

Each team in Group A plays the other three once. The top two advance to the FIBA AmeriCup 2029 Qualifiers, which begin in November 2027.

FIBA confirmed Jamaica's place in the competition after the team finished its campaign in the first round of the 2027 World Cup Americas Qualifiers. Follow our [Jamaica national team coverage](/category/national-team) for more.
