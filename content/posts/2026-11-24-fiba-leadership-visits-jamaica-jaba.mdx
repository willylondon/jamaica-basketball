Jamaica basketball hosted top international leadership in mid-November as FIBA Secretary General Andreas Zagklis and FIBA Americas CEO Carlos Alves completed a three-day official visit to the island from November 14 to 16, 2026.

Hosted by Jamaica Basketball Association (JaBA) President Paulton Gordon, the visit came on the heels of a milestone stretch for the senior men's national team, including Jamaica's debut campaign in the FIBA Basketball World Cup Americas Qualifiers and its first time hosting World Cup qualifying games at home.

## Meetings With Government and Local Stakeholders

During their stay, Zagklis, Alves, and Gordon held discussions with Jamaican government officials, the Jamaica Olympic Association, and local basketball stakeholders focused on strengthening partnerships and supporting domestic development across Jamaica's community and school programs.

The itinerary also included attending a Jamaica Basketball League showcase event at Campion College, underscoring the connection between grassroots competition and the island's international ambitions.

## Building on International Milestones

The FIBA leadership visit follows a breakthrough year for Jamaica's senior program, which featured [historic wins over Puerto Rico](/posts/jamaica-stuns-puerto-rico-fiba-qualifiers) and a landmark [FIBA World Cup Qualifiers campaign](/posts/jamaica-basketball-world-cup-qualifiers-recap).
