The Jamaica national basketball team opened the FIBA Basketball World Cup 2027 Americas Qualifiers with two wins over Puerto Rico. Both games were played on the road at the Coliseo Roberto Clemente in San Juan, on Friday, November 28 and Monday, December 1, 2025. They were the first World Cup qualifier games Jamaica had ever played.

## Why Jamaica played in San Juan

Jamaica was meant to host the opener. The Jamaica Basketball Association (JABA) could not meet FIBA's hosting requirements at a September 2025 inspection, so Puerto Rico staged the game, and FIBA reported that the effects of Hurricane Melissa forced Jamaica to play both games away. The team made its home debut in February at the National Indoor Sports Centre in Kingston.

## Game 1: Jamaica 92, Puerto Rico 90

Kofi Cockburn won it with a finish at the buzzer. He scored 26 points on 12-of-14 shooting and added 15 rebounds. Tyran De Lattibeaudiere scored 15 points, seven of them from the free-throw line. Andrew Thelwell had 12 points, seven rebounds and seven assists.

## Game 2: Jamaica 97, Puerto Rico 92

Three nights later, Jamaica led by as many as 20 in the second half before Puerto Rico cut the gap to three late. Romaine Thomas hit back-to-back threes to start an 11-2 second-quarter run and finished with 18 points. Chase Audige led Jamaica with 20, Cockburn added 15 points and 12 rebounds, and De Lattibeaudiere scored 15.

Jamaica made 13 of 27 three-pointers and 18 of 21 free throws, and Puerto Rico turned the ball over 21 times.

## What the sweep meant

- Jamaica went 2-0 and led Group B after the first window.
- Jamaica became the first team in these qualifiers to score 90 or more in each of its first two games.
- Puerto Rico had won nine of its previous ten home qualifiers and had never before lost both games in a window.

## How the campaign finished

The wins did not carry through the group. Jamaica lost its next four games: 99-96 to the Bahamas and 97-81 to Canada in Kingston, then 123-74 in Nassau and 116-78 to Canada in Hamilton. Jamaica finished 2-4 and did not advance. Read more in our [Jamaica national team coverage](/category/national-team).
