Chase Audige will join the Jamaica basketball team in Managua for the FIBA AmeriCup 2029 pre-qualifiers, his Spanish club San Pablo Burgos said on Monday.

Audige averaged 16.8 points and 3.8 assists in six games for Jamaica in the 2027 World Cup Americas Qualifiers, according to FIBA. Burgos’s announcement covers Audige’s plans for this window.

Jamaica opens against Costa Rica on Friday, November 27, at 5:30 p.m. Jamaica time. It plays Guyana at the same time on Saturday and Nicaragua at 8 p.m. on Sunday. The top two teams in Group A advance. [See the full Jamaica schedule](/posts/jamaica-basketball-americup-pre-qualifiers-managua).
