Sagicor B captured the 2026 Business House Basketball Association Men’s Division Two championship on Monday, November 23, edging the ATL Vikings 25–22 in Game 5 at the National Stadium Courts in Kingston. The victory completed a comeback from a 2–0 deficit in the best-of-five series, giving Sagicor B a 3–2 finals triumph in one of [Jamaica basketball’s](/posts/business-house-basketball-corporate-competition-meets-community) cornerstone corporate competitions.

## Game 5 decider and top performers

Sagicor B controlled the opening half of the low-scoring decider and took a 16–9 lead into the break, according to [IRIE FM’s match report](https://iriefm.net/sagicor-lifts-business-house-mens-division-2-basketball-title/). ATL responded after halftime, erasing the seven-point margin to tie the contest at 20–20 before Sagicor B pulled away late to seal the three-point win.

Adrian Wellington paced Sagicor B with eight points and 10 rebounds, while Paul Sutherland filled the box score with eight points, 16 rebounds, eight assists, and two steals. Captain Christopher Harris told IRIE FM he was overjoyed at the team’s collective achievement. Vaughn Chevannes led the ATL Vikings in scoring with nine points and seven rebounds.

## How the 3–2 series turned

The ATL Vikings—fielding employees from across the ATL Group, including Appliance Traders Limited, ATL Automotive, and the Jamaica Observer, under coach Nyron Hurd and manager Khenielle Cooper—entered their first Business House final since joining the league in 2024 and opened a commanding 2–0 series lead, as reported by the [Jamaica Observer](https://www.jamaicaobserver.com/2026/11/16/atl-vikings-eye-first-business-house-basketball-title/).

Sagicor B stayed alive by taking Game 3 and then leveled the series with a narrow 27–25 victory in Game 4 to force Monday night’s winner-take-all finale.

## West edges East in the All-Stars final

Earlier on the same programme at the National Stadium Courts, the West team defeated the East team 33–30 in the Business House All-Stars final. Damian Richards led the West with 10 points.
