Jamaica basketball opens its FIBA AmeriCup 2029 Pre-Qualifiers campaign against Costa Rica on Friday, November 27, with head coach Rick Turner leaning on eight first-time senior national team players in Managua.

Speaking to the Jamaica Observer ahead of the Group A opener at the Polideportivo Alexis Argüello, Turner said the staff has had to compress preparation into a two-and-a-half-day training camp. Tip-off is scheduled for 5:30 p.m. Jamaica time (4:30 p.m. in Managua).

"We have eight first-time players. We're trying to cram a proper training camp into two and a half days, but everyone is working hard and, more importantly, really excited to be here and extremely proud to represent Jamaica," Turner told the Jamaica Observer.

## Jamaica national basketball team faces Costa Rica rematch

Turner warned that Costa Rica will arrive confident and eager to avenge its previous defeat to Jamaica. When the teams met in August 2025 during the FIBA Basketball World Cup 2027 Americas Pre-Qualifiers in Mexico, Jamaica needed overtime to win 80–76 behind [Norman Powell’s 34-point performance](/posts/norman-powell-jamaica-debut-34-points-costa-rica).

With Powell absent on NBA duty and recovering from a right hamstring strain in Chicago, Jamaica enters the Managua window with a reshaped rotation. Ahead of the tournament, Spanish club San Pablo Burgos announced [Chase Audige’s planned arrival in Managua](/posts/chase-audige-jamaica-basketball-americup-managua), while guard Andrew Thelwell and forward Tyran De Lattibeaudiere were earlier confirmed in the [Jamaica national basketball team AmeriCup preview](/posts/jamaica-americup-pre-qualifiers-preview).

## FIBA AmeriCup 2029 pre-qualifiers schedule and stakes

Jamaica plays three games in three consecutive days in Group A at the Polideportivo Alexis Argüello:

- **Friday, November 27 at 5:30 p.m. Jamaica time** — Jamaica vs. Costa Rica
- **Saturday, November 28 at 5:30 p.m. Jamaica time** — Guyana vs. Jamaica
- **Sunday, November 29 at 8 p.m. Jamaica time** — Nicaragua vs. Jamaica

Only the top two teams from Group A advance to the main FIBA AmeriCup 2029 Qualifiers, which begin in November 2027. See our [full Jamaica AmeriCup pre-qualifiers schedule](/posts/jamaica-basketball-americup-pre-qualifiers-managua) for competition details.
