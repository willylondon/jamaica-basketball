Unified basketball is on the programme at the Special Olympics Caribbean Beach Games in Montego Bay. The event is scheduled for November 18–22 at Harmony Beach Park, according to Special Olympics and the Jamaica Information Service.

The Jamaica Basketball Association’s November 19 update showed its president, Paulton Gordon, among stakeholders who visited the park during planning.

Special Olympics Unified Basketball brings people with and without intellectual disabilities together on the same team.
