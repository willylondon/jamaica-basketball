Jamaica youth international Triston McDonald has signed with the University of West Georgia’s NCAA Division I men’s basketball programme. The Wolves named him alongside Noah Brady and Lorenzo McCorvey in their November 13 signing announcement.

West Georgia said McDonald is joining from IX International. The university also noted that he has represented Jamaica at under-17 and under-20 level. His [FIBA player profile](https://www.fiba.basketball/en/players/309489-triston-mcdonald) lists Jamaica as his national team.
