Norman Powell scored 17 points as the Chicago Bulls beat the Brooklyn Nets 137–107 on Wednesday, November 18. Chicago’s game notes credit the Jamaica international with three made three-pointers in the opening quarter.

The road win took Chicago to 4–10 and gave the Bulls their third victory in four games.

Powell scored 34 for Jamaica in an overtime win over Costa Rica in August 2025, a result that secured Jamaica’s place in the FIBA Basketball World Cup 2027 Americas Qualifiers.
