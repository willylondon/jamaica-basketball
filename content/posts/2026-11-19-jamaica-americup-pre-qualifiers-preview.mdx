The Jamaica national basketball team opens its FIBA AmeriCup 2029 pre-qualifying campaign against Costa Rica in Managua on Friday, November 27. Jamaica plays Guyana on Saturday and host Nicaragua on Sunday. The top two teams in Group A advance to the AmeriCup 2029 Qualifiers, which begin in November 2027.

Jamaica is here after finishing 2-4 and fourth in its World Cup qualifying group. [Read how that campaign ended](/posts/jamaica-basketball-world-cup-qualifiers-recap).

## Jamaica squad news

As of November 19, the Jamaica Basketball Association (JABA) had not published its full 12-man squad, and FIBA's team page did not yet list a roster. We'll update this preview when the squad is named.

What has been confirmed:

- **Rick Turner** returns as head coach. He led Jamaica through the World Cup qualifiers.
- **Andrew Thelwell** has committed. The guard led Jamaica in scoring in both July qualifiers, with 24 points in Nassau and 25 in Hamilton. In the opening win over Puerto Rico he had 12 points, seven rebounds and seven assists.
- **Tyran De Lattibeaudiere** has also committed. He scored 15 points in each of the two wins over Puerto Rico.

JABA has not said whether other regulars from the World Cup qualifiers will be available. That group includes Kofi Cockburn, Chase Audige, Romaine Thomas and Romaro Gill.

## The opponents

### Costa Rica: Friday, November 27, 5:30 p.m. Jamaica time

Costa Rica won the Central American pre-qualifier in March unbeaten, beating El Salvador 85-77 in the deciding game. Daniel Sheeden scored 20 points and Manrique Alvarado 18 in that win for coach Nicolás Marín.

The teams last met in August 2025, at the World Cup pre-qualifiers in Mexico. Jamaica won 80-76 in overtime behind [Norman Powell's 34 points](/posts/norman-powell-jamaica-debut-34-points-costa-rica).

### Guyana: Saturday, November 28, 5:30 p.m. Jamaica time

Guyana is a late entry. It took the place of Antigua and Barbuda, which withdrew after finishing in the top two of the Caribbean pre-qualifiers. Coach Alan Walls named a 12-man squad on November 11 with seven locally based players, four from overseas and the naturalized wing Umar Rashid. He said the staff picked for size and versatility.

"This roster represents the best available talent capable of competing at the FIBA level," Walls said.

### Nicaragua: Sunday, November 29, 8 p.m. Jamaica time

The hosts, like Jamaica, came through the World Cup qualifiers' first round without advancing. Nicaragua went 0-6 in a group with the United States, Mexico and the Dominican Republic. All three of its games in Managua will be in front of a home crowd at the Polideportivo Alexis Argüello.

## What Jamaica needs

Each team plays the other three once, and the top two advance. With the hosts closing out the group on Sunday night, Jamaica's first two games against Costa Rica and Guyana could decide its place before that last game.
