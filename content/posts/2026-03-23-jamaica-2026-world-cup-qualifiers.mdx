> **Update, November 19, 2026:** Jamaica finished the first round at 2-4 after losing 123-74 in Nassau and 116-78 to Canada in Hamilton in July, and did not advance.

The Jamaica national basketball team played its first official home games in February and March, hosting the Bahamas and Canada in the FIBA Basketball World Cup 2027 Americas Qualifiers. They were also the first top-level international basketball games held at the National Indoor Sports Centre in Kingston.

## Getting the arena ready

Getting there took work. In September 2025 the Jamaica Basketball Association (JABA) could not meet FIBA's hosting requirements at an inspection, so the November opener against Puerto Rico moved to San Juan. JABA then had to raise about $44 million for a modern scoreboard, new hoops and other upgrades to bring the venue up to FIBA standard, with Main Event Entertainment Group installing other components.

"When you look at the bigger picture in terms of what it can mean for basketball, this is why we do it," JABA president Paulton Gordon told the *Jamaica Observer*.

Jamaica came home 2-0 after the [sweep of Puerto Rico](/posts/jamaica-stuns-puerto-rico-fiba-qualifiers), level with Canada at the top of Group B.

## Jamaica vs Bahamas: 96-99

On February 26, a win would have sent Jamaica into the next round. Jamaica led 81-77 after three quarters, but the Bahamas won the fourth 22-15 in a game with 14 lead changes. Franco Miller scored 32 points, hitting six of 12 three-pointers, including a shot-clock three that put the Bahamas up 99-93 with 55 seconds left. Kofi Cockburn led Jamaica with 23 points.

"It was tough. We had to battle the entire time," Miller said. [Read the full Jamaica vs Bahamas recap](/posts/jamaica-falls-to-bahamas-fiba-world-cup-qualifier-2026).

## Jamaica vs Canada: 81-97

Three days later, on March 1, Canada won 97-81 to stay unbeaten in the group. [Read the full Jamaica vs Canada recap](/posts/canada-defeats-jamaica-fiba-world-cup-qualifier-2026).

## What it meant

The two home losses left Jamaica 2-2 heading into July's road games in Nassau and Hamilton. The lasting result is a national arena that has now hosted World Cup qualifiers, which gives JABA a FIBA-standard home court for future windows.
