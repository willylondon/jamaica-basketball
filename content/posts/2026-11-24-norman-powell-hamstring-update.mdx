The Chicago Bulls said Jamaica international Norman Powell suffered a mild right hamstring strain in their November 20 game against the Washington Wizards. Chicago expects to reassess the guard later this week. The team’s November 22 update gave no return date.

Powell missed Sunday’s game against Denver. The NBA’s Monday injury report also listed him out against Cleveland with the same strain. Before the injury, [he scored 17 points in Chicago’s win at Brooklyn](/posts/norman-powell-bulls-nets-november-2026).

Jamaica opens its FIBA AmeriCup 2029 pre-qualifying campaign against Costa Rica in Managua on Friday, November 27, at 5:30 p.m. Jamaica time. Guyana follows on Saturday and host Nicaragua on Sunday. [See all three tip-off times](/posts/jamaica-basketball-americup-pre-qualifiers-managua).
