> **Update, November 19, 2026:** Jamaica lost both games, 123-74 to the Bahamas in Nassau and 116-78 to Canada in Hamilton. The national team finished Group B at 2-4 and did not advance. The preview below has been corrected; see the note at the end.

After sweeping Puerto Rico in San Juan in November, the Jamaican national basketball team is back on the road. The FIBA Basketball World Cup 2027 Americas Qualifiers continue this July with two away games that close out the first round in Group B.

## The Schedule

Jamaica's Window 3 schedule:

- **July 3, 2026:** Bahamas vs Jamaica (Kendal Isaacs Gymnasium, Nassau)
- **July 6, 2026:** Canada vs Jamaica (TD Coliseum, Hamilton)

## The Stakes

Jamaica sits at 2-2 in Group B after splitting four games. The Bahamas beat Jamaica 99-96 in Kingston in February, and they will have their home crowd behind them in Nassau.

Three days later, Jamaica faces Canada, the strongest team in the group. Canada beat Jamaica 97-81 in Kingston in Window 2.
