Norman Powell scored 34 points as the Jamaica national basketball team beat Costa Rica 80-76 in overtime on August 9, 2025. The game was at the ITSON Arena in Ciudad Obregón, Mexico, at the FIBA Basketball World Cup 2027 Americas Pre-Qualifiers. It was Powell's second game for Jamaica.

## His Jamaica debut

Powell made his debut a day earlier, on August 8, against Barbados. He scored 12 points in 28 minutes as Jamaica won 101-79. Romaine Thomas led Jamaica with 20 points and 10 rebounds, and Romaro Gill added 19 points, 11 rebounds and four blocks.

## 34 points against Costa Rica

Against Costa Rica, Powell was the only Jamaican to reach double figures. He made five three-pointers and 11 free throws and added three steals and two assists. The game was tied 71-71 at the end of regulation. Jamaica won overtime 9-5, and Powell scored five of those nine points. Romaro Gill finished with eight points, 18 rebounds and three blocks.

## What the win meant

Jamaica needed a top-two finish in Group A to reach the Americas Qualifiers. The wins over Barbados and Costa Rica secured it before the final game. Powell did not play in that game, and hosts Mexico won 90-61.

Jamaica finished second in the group behind Mexico. That earned the team its first place in the FIBA World Cup Americas Qualifiers, where it was drawn with Canada, the Bahamas and Puerto Rico. [Read how that campaign unfolded](/category/national-team).
